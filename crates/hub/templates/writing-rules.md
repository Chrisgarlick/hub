# Writing rules

What must, mustn't or should appear in anything made for this client. One
rule per bullet, under a heading for where it applies: Everywhere, Website
copy, Reports and proposals, Boards, Social and video.

Start a rule with Don't, Never, Always, Avoid or Prefer. Put exact words in
"double quotes" and Hub checks them in drafts: `Don't mention "the old
brand".` Rules without quotes are guidance for Claude to follow.
